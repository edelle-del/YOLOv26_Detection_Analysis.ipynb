CROSS-MODEL COMPARISON
-------------------------------------------------------

Overall, Model 3 (auto) achieved the highest mAP50 of 0.8271,
indicating it generalized best across all four vehicle classes.

Precision vs Recall trade-off: Higher precision means fewer false positives
(the model is conservative in its detections), while higher recall means
fewer false negatives (the model detects more true instances). A balanced
F1 Score reflects how well a model handles both.

Classes such as Motorcycle and Bus tend to be harder to detect across all
models due to scale variation — motorcycles appear small at distance while
buses occupy large frame areas, both challenging standard anchor-based
detection at 640×640 resolution.

The per-class AP50 table reveals which optimizer and epoch configuration
best handles each vehicle type, and teams should consider class-weighted
loss or additional augmentation for consistently low-scoring classes.
